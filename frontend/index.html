How the grid makes a decision

01
Dots are countries

Each dot is a country node. Its size and color show the current screening pressure from flow imbalance and load residual.

02
Lines carry power

Moving particles show the direction of observed transfer. A thicker line means more of the observed-flow envelope is being used.

03
Colors are early warnings

Green has room, amber needs investigation, and red is the first link the scenario planner tries to relieve.

04
Rerouting is a test

The planner removes a chosen share, searches connected links, rejects unsafe headroom, and draws the best surviving path in purple.
